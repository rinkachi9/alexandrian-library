# Natural sciences

Disciplines that study nature through observation and experiment. Chemistry (biochemistry) and biology (cell biology) have notes; the other fields are planned.

## Fields

- [Physics](physics/README.md): Matter, energy, forces, space and time.
- [Chemistry](chemistry/README.md): The composition, structure and reactions of substances.
- [Biology](biology/README.md): Living organisms, from molecules to ecosystems.
- [Earth sciences](earth-sciences/README.md): The structure and processes of the planet.
- [Astronomy](astronomy/README.md): Celestial objects and the universe as a whole.

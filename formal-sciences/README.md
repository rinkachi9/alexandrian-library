# Formal sciences

Disciplines that study abstract structures and rules of reasoning instead of the physical world. This area is planned and has no notes yet.

## Fields

- [Mathematics](mathematics/README.md): Numbers, structures, space and change.
- [Logic](logic/README.md): Valid reasoning, proof and the structure of arguments.
- [Statistics and probability](statistics-and-probability/README.md): Describing uncertainty and drawing conclusions from data.
- [Theoretical computer science](theoretical-computer-science/README.md): What can be computed, how fast and with how much memory.
- [Decision and systems theory](decision-and-systems-theory/README.md): Choice under constraints and the behavior of interacting parts.

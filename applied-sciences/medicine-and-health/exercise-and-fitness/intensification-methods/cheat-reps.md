# Cheat repetitions

## Summary

Cheat repetitions are one or two extra repetitions at the end of a set performed with a deliberate, small deviation from strict technique (for example a little body swing) or with help from a training partner. The aim is to increase muscle tension past the point of concentric failure. The method is often misunderstood and misused: it is not a license for poor technique throughout the set.

## Prerequisites

- [Repetition ranges and load](../training-variables/rep-ranges-and-load.md)

## Core concepts

### Definition

*Cheat repetitions* are a few extra repetitions completed with controlled momentum or partner assistance after strict repetitions have reached failure. When a partner helps, they are often called *forced repetitions*.

### Analogy

Cheat repetitions are like a friend giving your car a small push when it stalls on a hill. You get a bit further than you could alone. The analogy breaks if the push becomes the engine: if every repetition is cheated, the muscle no longer does the work.

### Mechanism

At concentric failure, the muscle can still produce substantial force in the eccentric phase. A small amount of momentum or assistance gets the load past the sticking point, and the muscle controls the lowering. This adds high-effort repetitions to the set.

Cheat repetitions should be:

- limited to the last one or two repetitions;
- used on safe exercises (curls, lateral raises, machine work);
- done with a controlled lowering.

### Claimed effects and evidence

Each entry states the claimed effect and how strong the evidence in trained or untrained adults is, using the grades defined in the [Evidence grades](../../nutrition/evidence-grades.md) note.

- **Additional hypertrophy from forced repetitions.** Small or inconsistent effects in research. Evidence: limited.
- **Higher fatigue and injury risk if misused.** Momentum on heavy compound lifts increases joint stress. Evidence: established physiology.

## Worked example

A lifter does strict barbell curls with 35 kg to failure at 9 repetitions. They then do 2 more with a slight hip drive to get the bar past the sticking point, lowering each over 3 seconds. The set ends there.

## Trade offs and when to use it

Cheat repetitions can extend a set safely on simple exercises, but they increase fatigue and are dangerous on heavy compound lifts.

## Common mistakes

- **Cheating from the first repetition.** Use strict form until failure.
- **Cheating on heavy squats or deadlifts.** Use it only on safe exercises.

## Practice

1. What are cheat repetitions?
2. What is the most common misuse?
3. Which exercises suit cheat repetitions?

### Answers

1. One or two extra repetitions with slight momentum or partner help after strict failure.
2. Using momentum on every repetition.
3. Simple exercises such as curls, lateral raises and machines.

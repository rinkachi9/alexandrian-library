# Applied sciences

Fields that use knowledge from the other domains to solve practical problems and run professions. Medicine and health, business and management, and finance and investing (corporate finance) have notes; computer science and engineering are planned.

## Fields

- [Medicine and health](medicine-and-health/README.md): The human body, its care and its performance.
- [Computer science](computer-science/README.md): Building, running and securing computing systems. The mathematical theory sits in theoretical computer science under formal sciences.
- [Engineering](engineering/README.md): Designing and building physical and technical systems.
- [Business and management](business-and-management/README.md): Creating, running and growing organizations.
- [Finance and investing](finance-and-investing/README.md): Managing money, markets and risk.

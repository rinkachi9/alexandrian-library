# Finance and investing

Managing money, markets and risk. This field belongs to [Applied sciences](../README.md). The written part so far is corporate finance: how a company measures its profit and cash, values itself and decides which investments to make. Personal finance and investing in markets are planned.

## Subfields

- [Corporate finance](corporate-finance/README.md): financial statements, the time value of money, EBITDA, operating and capital expenditure, free cash flow, discounted cash flow valuation, WACC, NPV, IRR and the payback period.

## Planned topics

- Personal finance
- Financial instruments
- Stocks
- Derivatives and options
- Portfolio construction
- Taxes and retirement

Business metrics for subscription and online businesses (MRR, CAC, LTV, ROI, ROAS) are in [business metrics](../business-and-management/business-metrics/README.md).

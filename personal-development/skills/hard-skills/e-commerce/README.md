# E-commerce

Selling goods and services online: transaction models, choosing and validating products, and the two main ways of sourcing them, dropshipping and private label. This subfield belongs to [Hard skills](../README.md). The notes were migrated from the e-commerce section of the Skills export and extended with sources, including the EU and US rules that changed the model in 2021 to 2026.

## Notes in reading order

1. [E-commerce models](e-commerce-models.md): definition, B2B, B2C, C2C, C2B and administration, the parts of an online business and the size of the market.
2. [Product selection](product-selection.md): criteria of a winning product, niches and unit economics with break-even ROAS.
3. [Product validation](product-validation.md): methods ordered by strength of evidence, and why stated intent is weak.
4. [Dropshipping](dropshipping.md): order flow, levels, responsibility toward customers and cross-border tax and customs rules.
5. [Private label](private-label.md): selling under your own brand, samples, honest demand tests and the manufacturer's obligations.

Search visibility for shops is in [E-commerce SEO](../search-engine-optimization/e-commerce-seo.md). Persuasive product pages are in [Copywriting](../copywriting/README.md).

These notes are educational. They describe rules in general terms and are not legal or tax advice.

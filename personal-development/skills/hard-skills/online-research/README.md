# Online research

Finding information online efficiently and judging whether it can be trusted: search strategy, search operators, source evaluation, specialized databases, verifying images and video, and using AI tools safely. This skill belongs to [Hard skills](../README.md). The notes were migrated from the Google tips in the "Research" section of the Skills export, checked against current Google documentation, and extended with research on how experts evaluate information.

## Notes in reading order

1. [Search strategy](search-strategy.md): defining the question, information foraging, building search terms, Boolean logic, recording and when to stop.
2. [Search operators](search-operators.md): operators that work in Google, the source tips with their current status, and operators that no longer work.
3. [Source evaluation](source-evaluation.md): lateral reading, SIFT, CRAAP and its limits, strength of evidence and red flags.
4. [Specialized sources](specialized-sources.md): research literature, official statistics, law, company registers, archives and standards.
5. [Image and media verification](image-and-media-verification.md): reverse image search, metadata, geolocation, provenance standards and AI-generated media.
6. [Research with AI tools](research-with-ai-tools.md): good and risky uses, fabricated citations and a verification routine.

## How to learn it

Read notes 1 to 3 and apply them to the next question you need to answer for work. Practice lateral reading on claims you see in your feed. Learn the two or three specialized sources most relevant to your field (note 4). Read notes 5 and 6 before sharing images or using AI-generated research in your own work.

# Social media

Using social platforms to reach an audience, build relationships and lead people to action, through organic content, community work and paid advertising. This skill belongs to [Hard skills](../README.md). The source export has a "Social media" heading with only LinkedIn profile tips, so the notes were written from platform documentation, law and research, and the LinkedIn tips were kept in [LinkedIn for professionals](linkedin-for-professionals.md) and [professional profile](../../../career/job-search/professional-profile.md).

## Notes in reading order

1. [Social media marketing fundamentals](social-media-marketing-fundamentals.md): definition, platforms, goals, organic reach, listening and rented audiences.
2. [Platforms and algorithms](platforms-and-algorithms.md): how recommendation systems rank content, signals, formats and EU transparency rules.
3. [Social content creation](social-content-creation.md): content pillars, hooks, short video structure, production routine, accessibility and rights.
4. [Community management](community-management.md): responding, moderating, guidelines, participation inequality and crises.
5. [Paid social advertising](paid-social-advertising.md): campaign structure, objectives, audiences, creative, tracking after ATT and testing products with ads.
6. [Influencer marketing](influencer-marketing.md): choosing creators, agreements, disclosure law in the EU and US, and measurement.
7. [LinkedIn for professionals](linkedin-for-professionals.md): profile, networking, posting, company pages and social selling.
8. [Social media analytics](social-media-analytics.md): metrics, engagement rate formulas, matching metrics to goals and pitfalls.

## How to learn it

Read notes 1 and 2, choose one platform, and publish for four weeks using note 3 while replying to every comment (note 4). Review with note 8. Add paid campaigns (note 5) or creators (note 6) once organic content shows what resonates. Professionals and B2B sellers should start with note 7.

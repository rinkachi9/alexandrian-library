# Copywriting

Writing that makes a specific reader take a specific action, the research and strategy behind it, the principles of persuasion it relies on, and how to test whether it works. This skill belongs to [Hard skills](../README.md). The source export contains only a heading and the value "Obsessed" for copywriting, plus a lesson on prompting action, so most of the material was added from sources.

## Notes in reading order

1. [Copywriting fundamentals](copywriting-fundamentals.md): definition, direct response and brand, a short history of measured advertising, the process, elements of persuasive copy and legal limits.
2. [Customer research](customer-research.md): voice of customer, review mining, interviews, jobs to be done and Schwartz's awareness levels.
3. [Value proposition and benefits](value-proposition-and-benefits.md): features, benefits and outcomes, the value proposition canvas, kinds of proof and positioning against alternatives.
4. [Copywriting frameworks](copywriting-frameworks.md): AIDA, PAS, BAB, 4Ps, the five step formula and the anatomy of a sales page.
5. [Writing for the web](writing-for-the-web.md): scanning, structure, readability formulas, plain language, microcopy, editing and accessibility.
6. [Persuasion principles](persuasion-principles.md): Cialdini's principles, anchoring, loss aversion, the strength of the evidence and dark patterns.
7. [Calls to action and risk reversal](calls-to-action-and-risk-reversal.md): the last step of the formula, guarantees and what the law already requires.
8. [A/B testing](ab-testing.md): online controlled experiments, sample size and peeking.

## How to learn it

Start with notes 1 to 3 and rewrite three real product pages using them. Then practice one framework per week (note 4) and edit each draft with note 5. Read note 6 before using urgency or social proof. Use deliberate practice on headlines and openings (see [deliberate practice](../../deliberate-practice.md)), and test with real readers once traffic allows (note 8).

Headlines are covered in [content marketing](../content-marketing/headlines.md).

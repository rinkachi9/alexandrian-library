# Copywriting

Writing that makes a specific reader take a specific action, the principles of persuasion behind it, and how to test whether it works. This subfield belongs to [Hard skills](../README.md). The source export contains only a heading for copywriting and a lesson on prompting action, so most of the material was added from sources.

## Notes in reading order

1. [Copywriting](copywriting.md): research, features and benefits, AIDA and the five step formula, writing for screens.
2. [Persuasion principles](persuasion-principles.md): Cialdini's principles, anchoring, loss aversion, the strength of the evidence and dark patterns.
3. [Calls to action and risk reversal](calls-to-action-and-risk-reversal.md): the last step of the formula, guarantees and what the law already requires.
4. [A/B testing](ab-testing.md): online controlled experiments, sample size and peeking.

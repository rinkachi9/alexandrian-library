# Clinical and counseling psychology

Theories and methods used to understand and treat psychological difficulties. This subfield belongs to [Psychology](../README.md). Notes state the strength of the evidence for each approach. They are educational and are not clinical advice.

## Notes in reading order

1. [Object relations theory](object-relations-theory.md): a psychoanalytic theory of how early relationships shape the mind, with its evidence and its link to attachment.
2. [Neuro-linguistic programming](neuro-linguistic-programming.md): the techniques, the claims and what the research found.
3. [NLP metaprograms](nlp-metaprograms.md): the NLP typology of information filters and the researched constructs that resemble it.
4. [Cognitive behavioral therapy and cognitive distortions](cognitive-behavioral-therapy.md): the cognitive model, common distortions and the evidence.
5. [Psychodynamic schools](psychodynamic-schools.md): Freud, Adler and Jung compared.
6. [Trauma bonding](trauma-bonding.md): attachment to an abuser and the role of intermittent kindness.
7. [Compulsive sexual behavior](compulsive-sexual-behavior.md): the ICD-11 diagnosis, and when high use is not a disorder.
8. [Popular psychology labels](popular-psychology-labels/README.md): seven named patterns that are not diagnoses, such as the Atlas complex and the Cassandra complex, with their origin, the research behind them and the evidence status.

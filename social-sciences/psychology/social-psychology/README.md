# Social psychology

How people think about themselves and act in the presence of others. This subfield belongs to [Psychology](../README.md).

## Notes in reading order

1. [Self-verification theory](self-verification-theory.md): why people seek feedback that fits their self-view.
2. [Milgram obedience experiments](milgram-obedience-experiments.md): the study of obedience to authority, its variations, ethics and critics.
3. [Social deduction games](social-deduction-games.md): Werewolf as a model of deception, trust and group decisions, with a baseline calculation.
4. [Attribution biases](attribution-biases.md): the fundamental attribution error and the self-serving bias.
5. [Egocentric biases in social judgment](egocentric-biases.md): the spotlight effect, the illusion of transparency and false consensus.
6. [Liking gap and pratfall effect](liking-gap-and-pratfall-effect.md): first impressions that run against intuition.
7. [Expectation effects](expectation-effects.md): Pygmalion, Golem and self-fulfilling prophecies.
8. [Social comparison and the looking-glass self](social-comparison.md): self-evaluation through others.
9. [Stereotypes](stereotypes.md): categorization, automatic activation and control.
10. [Dark triad and manipulation](dark-triad.md): three traits and the tactics of manipulation.

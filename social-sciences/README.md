# Social sciences

Disciplines that study human behavior, societies and institutions. Law (Polish company law), economics, psychology and political science have notes; the other fields are planned.

## Fields

- [Psychology](psychology/README.md): Mind and behavior.
- [Economics](economics/README.md): How people and societies allocate scarce resources.
- [Sociology](sociology/README.md): Social structures, groups and institutions.
- [Political science](political-science/README.md): Power, government and public policy.
- [Law](law/README.md): Legal systems, rules and their application.
- [Anthropology](anthropology/README.md): Human cultures and human origins.
- [Education and learning](education-and-learning/README.md): How people learn and how learning is organized.
- [Communication and media](communication-and-media/README.md): How messages are made, carried and received.

# Microeconomics

How individuals, households and firms make choices under scarcity and how those choices meet in markets. This subfield belongs to [Economics](../README.md). The notes cover economic value, which underlies pricing, consumer and producer surplus and the valuation methods used in [finance](../../../applied-sciences/finance-and-investing/README.md).

## Notes in reading order

1. [Economic value](economic-value.md): utility, willingness to pay, opportunity cost, surplus and the kinds of value.
2. [Supply and demand](supply-and-demand.md): the laws of demand and supply, equilibrium, shifts, elasticity and price controls.
3. [Capital](capital.md): the factors of production, physical, human and financial capital, and accumulation.

# Humanities

Disciplines that study human thought, culture and expression through interpretation and critique. This area is planned and has no notes yet.

## Fields

- [Philosophy](philosophy/README.md): Fundamental questions about knowledge, existence, value and reasoning.
- [History](history/README.md): The human past and how it is reconstructed.
- [Linguistics and languages](linguistics-and-languages/README.md): Structure of language and the study of individual languages.
- [Literature](literature/README.md): Written works and their analysis.
- [Religion and mythology](religion-and-mythology/README.md): Belief systems, sacred texts and myth.
- [Archaeology](archaeology/README.md): Past societies studied through material remains.

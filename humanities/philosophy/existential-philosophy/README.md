# Existential philosophy

Philosophical answers to the question of meaning in a world that gives no ready answer. This subfield belongs to [Philosophy](../README.md). The notes move from the problem (nihilism) to two responses (existentialism and absurdism).

## Notes in reading order

1. [Nihilism](nihilism.md): the kinds of nihilism and Nietzsche's diagnosis.
2. [Existentialism](existentialism.md): freedom, responsibility and authenticity.
3. [Absurdism](absurdism.md): Camus, the absurd and revolt.

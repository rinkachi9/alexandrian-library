# Philosophy

Fundamental questions about knowledge, existence, value and reasoning. This field belongs to [Humanities](../README.md) The written subfields cover ancient philosophy, existential philosophy, Japanese thought and ethics.

## Subfields

1. [Ancient philosophy](ancient-philosophy/README.md): Greek psychology and Stoicism.
2. [Existential philosophy](existential-philosophy/README.md): nihilism, existentialism and absurdism.
3. [Japanese thought](japanese-thought/README.md): aesthetics, learning, endurance and proverbs.
4. [Ethics](ethics/README.md): moral dilemmas of artificial intelligence.
